Privacy Policy

PDFECTA is designed with privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains what data we do — and do not — collect when you use our browser-based PDF tools.

No File Uploads

PDFECTA processes PDF files entirely within your web browser. Your documents are never uploaded to our servers, stored remotely, or transmitted over the internet. All actions such as signing, merging, or editing PDFs occur locally on your device.

No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, or uploaded documents during normal use of PDFECTA tools. You can use our services without creating an account or providing identifying information.

Email Communication

If you choose to contact PDFECTA via email, your message and email address are used solely to respond to your inquiry. Messages are not analyzed for marketing purposes, added to mailing lists, sold, or shared with third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

PDFECTA does not use tracking cookies, behavioral analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking scripts. We do not monitor user behavior or build usage profiles.

Third-Party Libraries

Some PDF tools rely on open-source client-side libraries (such as PDF processing libraries). These libraries run locally in your browser and do not transmit data externally. PDFECTA does not embed third-party services that collect user data.

Data Security

Because files never leave your device, PDFECTA minimizes exposure to data breaches and unauthorized access. Security depends on your local device and browser environment, and we recommend keeping your system up to date.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements or changes to PDFECTA. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how PDFECTA handles data, contact us at support@pdfecta.com .
