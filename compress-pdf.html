How Our Compression Works

PDFECTA uses canvas-based rendering to compress PDF files. Each page of your PDF is rendered at high resolution and re-encoded as an optimized image. This approach achieves real file size reduction — especially effective on scanned documents, image-heavy PDFs, and files exported from design software.

The compression level you choose controls the balance between file size and visual quality. For most documents, Balanced compression is the best choice — it significantly reduces file size while keeping text sharp and images clear.

Compression Levels Explained

  • Balanced (Recommended): Good quality with significant size reduction. Best for most documents including reports, contracts, and presentations.
  • Low Compression: Maximum visual quality with moderate size reduction. Best for documents where image sharpness is critical.
  • High Compression: Maximum size reduction with some reduction in image sharpness. Best when file size is the priority over visual quality.

When Should You Compress a PDF?

Compression is useful whenever you need to upload files to websites with size limits or send documents through email. Many email providers limit attachments to around 20–25 MB, which scanned PDFs can easily exceed. Compressing a PDF ensures faster uploads, smoother sharing, and better compatibility with document management systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to compress PDF files here?

Yes. All compression happens directly in your browser. Your files are never uploaded or stored anywhere.

Does compression reduce PDF quality?

PDFECTA re-encodes PDF pages as optimized images. Higher compression levels reduce image quality slightly but text and layout remain readable. Choose Balanced for the best quality-to-size ratio.

What types of PDFs work best with compression?

Image-heavy PDFs and scanned documents see the largest size reductions. Text-only PDFs may see more modest reductions since they contain less image data to compress.

Is there a file size limit?

Performance depends on your device memory. Most modern devices can handle standard PDF files without issues. Very large PDFs may take longer to process.

Is this PDF compressor free?

Yes. You can compress PDF files online free with no registration required and no usage limits.
