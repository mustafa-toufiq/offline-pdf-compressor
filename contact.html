Contact PDFECTA

PDFECTA is built around privacy, transparency, and simplicity — and the same principles apply to how you contact us. We intentionally avoid contact forms, tracking scripts, and unnecessary data collection.

If you have a question, need support, or want to share feedback, email is the most direct and privacy-respecting way to reach us.

This address is monitored regularly and is used for all communication related to PDFECTA. Typical response time is within 24–48 hours.

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Questions about how PDFECTA tools work
  • Bug reports or unexpected behavior

Why We Don’t Use Contact Forms

Many websites rely on contact forms that store messages in databases, log IP addresses, or route data through third-party services. PDFECTA intentionally avoids this approach.

By using email instead of forms, we reduce data collection, avoid unnecessary tracking, and give users full control over what information they choose to share.

Privacy and Communication

When you email PDFECTA, your message is used only to respond to your inquiry. Messages are not analyzed, profiled, sold, or added to mailing lists.

Contacting PDFECTA does not require creating an account, accepting cookies, or agreeing to tracking. This approach aligns with our broader commitment to privacy-first PDF tools and minimal data handling.

How to Contact PDFECTA

PDFECTA provides a simple and privacy-respecting way to get in touch. We intentionally avoid contact forms, third-party widgets, and tracking scripts. Email is the only communication channel we use, ensuring clarity, transparency, and minimal data handling.

When you contact PDFECTA, your message is delivered directly to our support inbox. Messages are not stored in databases, analyzed for marketing purposes, or shared with third parties. We respond manually and only use your email address to reply to your inquiry.
