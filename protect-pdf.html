Add Password Protection to PDF Online Free & Secure

PDFECTA allows you to password protect PDF files instantly without uploading your document to any server. Whether you need to secure a contract, financial document, personal file, or confidential report, this free PDF protection tool provides fast and private encryption directly in your browser.

Unlike traditional online PDF tools that require file uploads, PDFECTA processes everything locally using modern browser technology. Your PDF file and your password never leave your device.

How to Password Protect a PDF File

  1. Upload or drag and drop your PDF file into the tool above.
  2. Enter a strong password in the password field.
  3. Confirm your password in the second field.
  4. Click "Protect PDF".
  5. Download your password-protected PDF instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to password protect a PDF online?

Yes. PDFECTA protects your PDF entirely in your browser. Your file and password are never uploaded to any server.

What type of encryption does PDFECTA use?

PDFECTA uses 128-bit RC4 encryption, compatible with all modern PDF readers including Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, and mobile apps.

Can I open the protected PDF on any device?

Yes. The password-protected PDF works on all devices and PDF readers. Anyone opening the file will be prompted to enter the password.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no strict limit. Performance depends on your device memory. Most modern devices handle standard PDF files without issues.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes. Open the protected PDF in Adobe Acrobat with your password, then re-save it without a password to remove the protection.
