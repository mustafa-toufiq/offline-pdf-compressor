Sign PDF vs Digital Signature — What’s the Difference?

PDFECTA adds a visual handwritten signature to your PDF document. This is ideal for approvals, agreements, and informal documentation. However, it is different from certificate-based digital signatures that use cryptographic encryption.

If you require government-grade or legally certified digital signing with authentication certificates, you would need specialized digital signature software. For everyday use such as signing forms, approvals, and personal documents, a handwritten-style PDF signature is often sufficient and widely accepted.
