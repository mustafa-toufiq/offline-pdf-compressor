Electronic Signature vs Digital Signature

PDFECTA adds a visual handwritten-style electronic signature to your PDF. This is different from certificate-based digital signatures that use cryptographic encryption to verify identity. An electronic signature is legally valid for most everyday documents including contracts, agreements, consent forms, and authorization letters in many countries. For documents specifically requiring certified cryptographic digital signatures, a specialized legal signing service would be needed.

Is an Online PDF Signature Legally Valid?

In most countries and jurisdictions, electronic signatures on PDF documents are legally valid and enforceable for everyday contracts and agreements. The United States, European Union, United Kingdom, and many other countries have legislation recognizing electronic signatures as legally equivalent to handwritten signatures for most purposes. However specific document types such as wills, deeds, and certain financial instruments may require wet ink or certified digital signatures — always check the requirements for your specific document type.
