How to Convert PNG to PDF Online — Free & Private

Screenshots, UI designs, logos, and scanned graphics are usually saved as PNG. But when you need to share them professionally, email them, or submit them to a portal, PDF is required. In this guide, you will learn how to convert PNG to PDF privately in your browser without uploading your images to any server.

Why Convert PNG to PDF?

PNG is great for quality but not ideal for sharing documents. PDF keeps layout consistent across all devices and is accepted everywhere.

  • Submit screenshots for assignments and job applications
  • Share designs and mockups with clients
  • Combine multiple screenshots into one report
  • Print images without quality loss or layout shifts
  • Create a portfolio from PNG artwork
  • Reduce file handling issues on mobile and email

JPG vs PNG to PDF: Which Should You Use?

JPG is better for photos and camera pictures. PNG is better for screenshots, graphics with text, logos, and images with transparency. If your image has sharp text or lines, PNG to PDF will keep it much clearer than JPG.

  • Use PNG to PDF for screenshots, Canva designs, and UI mockups
  • Use JPG to PDF for camera photos and scanned documents
  • PNG preserves text sharpness better

Will Transparent Background Stay Transparent?

PDF readers handle transparency inconsistently. To ensure your PDF opens correctly everywhere and prints properly, PDFECTA converts transparent areas to a clean white background. This keeps your final document professional and compatible.
