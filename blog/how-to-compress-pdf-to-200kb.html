How to Compress PDF to 200KB for Government Forms

Applying on FPSC, NTS, PPSC, passport portal, or university admission? Most Pakistan government portals reject PDFs above 200KB or 500KB. Your CNIC scan, domicile, and certificates are often 2-5MB after scanning. In this guide, you will learn how to compress PDF to 200KB, 100KB, and 500KB privately without uploading your ID documents to a server.

Why Portals Require Small PDFs

Government and job portals handle millions of applications. Small file limits help them save storage and load documents faster.

  • FPSC and PPSC usually require PDF under 500KB
  • NTS and university portals often require under 200KB or 300KB
  • Passport and NADRA portals require 100KB to 300KB for photos and documents
  • Large files fail to upload on slow mobile internet
  • Small PDFs are verified faster by the system

  5. If still large, delete blank pages using Delete Pages then compress again.
  6. Download and upload to portal.

How to Get to 100KB for Photos and CNIC

  • Scan at 150 DPI instead of 300 DPI for forms
  • Convert color scans to black & white if allowed
  • Remove second side if portal asks for front only
  • Crop extra white borders before compressing
  • Use JPG to PDF with 70% quality then compress

Best Files to Compress for Job Portals

  • CNIC front and back scans
  • Domicile and educational certificates
  • Passport size photos converted to PDF
  • Experience letters and NOC
  • Combined application PDFs

Tips to Get Below 200KB Faster

  • Delete blank pages first — they add size
  • Compress before merging, not after
  • Scan in grayscale for text documents
  • Avoid scanning at very high resolution
  • Use strong compression for government forms — readability matters more than color
