    PNG vs JPG — Which Should You Convert to PDF?

    Both PNG and JPG images can be converted to PDF using PDFECTA. The key difference is that PNG uses lossless compression, meaning it preserves full image quality — making it the best choice for screenshots, graphics, logos, and documents with sharp text. JPG uses lossy compression, resulting in smaller file sizes but slightly reduced quality, making it better suited for photographs.

    If your images are in JPG format, use our JPG to PDF converter instead. Both tools work identically and process everything locally in your browser.
