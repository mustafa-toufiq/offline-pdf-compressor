Extract Pages from PDF Online — Free, Secure & Private

PDFECTA allows you to extract specific pages from a PDF file directly in your browser. You can save selected pages, separate certain sections, or create a new PDF from chosen page ranges. Everything runs locally using client-side processing — your documents never leave your computer.

When Should You Extract Pages from a PDF?

  • Remove confidential or unnecessary sections
  • Share only selected chapters of a document
  • Create smaller files for email attachments
  • Prepare documents for printing
  • Submit only required pages for applications

How to Extract Specific Pages from a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Enter the page numbers or ranges (e.g., 1–3, 7, 10–12).
  3. Click Extract Pages Now.
  4. Download your new PDF instantly.

Extract Pages vs Split PDF — What’s the Difference?

Extracting pages allows you to select specific pages and save them into a new PDF. Splitting a PDF typically divides the entire document into multiple separate files. If you only need certain pages, extracting is faster and more precise.
