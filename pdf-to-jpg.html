PDF to JPG vs PDF to PNG — Which to Choose?

JPG uses lossy compression resulting in smaller file sizes but slightly reduced quality. PNG uses lossless compression preserving full quality but producing larger files. For most use cases — social media, email, presentations — JPG is the better choice. For documents with sharp text or logos where pixel-perfect quality matters, consider PNG format instead.
