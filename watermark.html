What Is a PDF Watermark?

A watermark is visible text placed over a document page to indicate status or ownership. Common examples include “CONFIDENTIAL”, “DRAFT”, “SAMPLE”, “COPY”, or company branding labels. Watermarks help protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized redistribution.

With this online watermark PDF tool, you can add consistent watermark text across all pages of your document in seconds.
